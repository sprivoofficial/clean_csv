How it works

Clean Sheet turns the file you download from your bank or credit card into one that QuickBooks Online will import.

  1. 1Drop your file

    The CSV you downloaded from your bank or card.

  2. 2We clean and check it

    Junk rows out, dates and amounts tidied. Every change is shown.

What we fix

  • Title lines and total rows
  • Blank rows
  • Extra spaces
  • Mixed date styles
  • Amounts like (12.50) or 45.00 DR
  • Debit and Credit columns
  • Credit card purchases that look like deposits

Will my file work?

For English-language bank and card exports. One account per file; if a file has several currencies, you pick one at a time. Tested on 40 made-up sample files that imitate common layouts. Every bank is a little different, so look over the result before you upload it to QuickBooks.

Supported

  • A .csv or .txt file (commas, semicolons, tabs or pipes)
  • Checking, savings and credit-card exports in the usual layouts
  • One Amount column, or separate Debit and Credit columns
  • Credit-card files with positive purchases: you confirm the sign before converting
  • Title lines, blank rows, repeated headers and total rows are set aside and listed, so you can check them
  • Dates like 02/16/2026, 2026-02-16, Feb 16 2026, 16-Feb-26. Unclear ones like 03/04/2026: we ask month first or day first
  • Files with two dates (transaction and posting): we ask which one to use
  • Amounts like $1,234.56, (12.50), 45.00 DR, 45.00-. When the meaning is unclear, we ask
  • Accented letters in UTF-8, UTF-16 or Windows-1252 files
  • Large exports are split into files of up to 1,000 rows

Experimental

  • PayPal and Wise exports: checked against sample files published online
  • Stripe exports: layout not yet checked against a real Stripe file
  • Check amounts, fees and the currency carefully before you upload

Not supported

  • Excel (.xlsx): export the transactions sheet as CSV first
  • PDF statements and images: download a CSV from your bank instead
  • QBO and OFX files: outside this tool’s scope (it converts CSV and TXT files)
  • Files that are not in English
  • QuickBooks Desktop: this makes files for QuickBooks Online

Questions

Why won’t my bank CSV import into QuickBooks Online?

QuickBooks wants a plain table: either Date, Description and Amount, or Date, Description, Credit and Debit, with one date style all the way down. Bank files often have title lines, blank rows, total rows, extra columns and mixed date styles. Clean Sheet removes or sets those aside, lists everything it set aside, and checks the finished file before you download it.

Why do my credit card purchases show up as deposits?

Many card files list purchases as positive numbers, and QuickBooks reads a positive number as money coming in. Clean Sheet notices when almost every amount is positive and asks whether to flip the signs. It shows your total money in and money out so you can compare them with your statement. It never flips on its own.

Is my bank file uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is read and cleaned inside this browser tab. The page is built so that your browser blocks every network connection, and it loads nothing from other sites. You can turn off your internet and it keeps working.

My file has more than 1,000 rows. What now?

QuickBooks takes up to 1,000 rows in one upload. Clean Sheet splits a longer file into parts of up to 1,000 rows each, with the header on every part, so you can upload them one after another.

Which date format should I choose?

Next to the download button you choose month first (02/16/2026) or day first (16/02/2026). Pick the one you will select in QuickBooks when it asks for the date format during the upload. The two must match.

Which banks does it work with, and why is it a Beta?

It reads the common layouts: one Amount column, or separate Debit and Credit columns. It has only been tested on made-up sample files, so it may stumble on a layout it has not seen. When it is unsure it asks, or leaves a row out and tells you why. PayPal, Stripe and Wise exports are experimental. Please check the result before importing it into QuickBooks.
